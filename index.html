Cost Modeling Methodology

How this estimator calculates costs — the formulas, assumptions, and frameworks behind the numbers.

Overview Staffing Facility Programming Nutrition Overhead Key Metrics Benchmarks References

Overview

This cost estimator uses an activity-based costing (ABC) approach adapted for nonprofit and publicly funded afterschool programs. Costs are organized into five direct-cost categories and one indirect-cost category:

Staffing Directors + Program Staff + Benefits
Facility Rent + Utilities + Custodial
Programming Materials + Activities + Tech + PD
Nutrition Snacks + Meals − Reimbursements
Operations Admin Overhead + Insurance + Transport
Total Annual Program Cost
Total = Staffing + Facility + Programming + Nutrition + Operations

Staffing Cost Formula

Staffing is typically the largest cost category, often representing 60–80% of total program costs. This model separates salaried leadership from hourly program staff.

Director/Coordinator Cost
Director Cost = (# Directors) × (Annual Salary) × (1 + Benefits Rate)
Program Staff Cost
Staff Cost = (# Staff) × (Hourly Wage) × (Paid Hours/Day) × (Days/Week) × (Weeks/Year) × (1 + Benefits Rate)
Total Staffing Cost
Staffing = Director Cost + Staff Cost

Key Assumptions

  • Benefits Rate covers employer-paid FICA (7.65%), health insurance, retirement contributions, and unemployment insurance. The national average for nonprofits is approximately 22–28%.
  • Paid hours per day should include setup and breakdown time beyond program hours (typically +30–60 minutes).
  • Directors are modeled as full-time equivalent (FTE) salaried employees; fractional FTE can be entered (e.g., 0.5 for half-time).

Facility Cost Formula

Facility costs include rent or space-use fees, utilities, and custodial services. School-based programs may receive space in-kind, which should be documented as both a cost and in-kind contribution for grant reporting purposes.

Annual Facility Cost
Facility = (Monthly Rent + Monthly Utilities + Monthly Custodial) × Months of Operation

Key Assumptions

  • Months of operation may differ from staffing weeks if facility costs are paid year-round.
  • In-kind space contributions should be valued at fair market rate and reported separately, even if cost to program is $0.
  • For shared facilities, prorate costs based on hours of use ÷ total building hours.

Programming Cost Formula

Programming costs are the direct costs of delivering educational and enrichment content to students.

Annual Programming Cost
Programming = (Materials/Student + Activities/Student) × Students Enrolled + Technology + Professional Development

Key Assumptions

  • Per-student costs for materials and activities are multiplied by enrolled students, not ADA, since these are typically purchased at the start of the year.
  • Technology and professional development are modeled as fixed annual costs.
  • Professional development includes training, conferences, and certification costs for all staff.

Nutrition Cost Formula

Nutrition costs are calculated based on average daily attendance (ADA) rather than enrollment, since food is purchased daily.

Gross Nutrition Cost
Gross Nutrition = (Snack Cost + Meal Cost*) × ADA × Operating Days *Meal cost applied only if meals are provided
Net Nutrition Cost
Net Nutrition = Gross Nutrition − CACFP Reimbursement

Key Assumptions

  • The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) provides federal reimbursements for qualifying meals and snacks. Programs should apply if serving income-eligible students.
  • CACFP reimbursement rates (FY2025): ~$0.13–$0.32/snack and ~$3.48–$4.15/meal depending on tier and income eligibility.
  • Net negative nutrition costs (reimbursements exceed costs) are capped at $0 in the total.

Operations & Overhead Formula

Administrative overhead accounts for the indirect costs of running the program — fiscal management, human resources, IT, and organizational support.

Administrative Overhead
Admin Overhead = (Staffing + Facility + Programming + Nutrition) × Overhead Rate
Total Operations Cost
Operations = Admin Overhead + Insurance + Transportation + Marketing + Evaluation

Key Assumptions

  • The overhead rate represents the percentage of direct costs allocated to administrative functions. Funders often cap this at 10–20%.
  • Insurance includes general liability, professional liability, and accident coverage for participants.
  • Transportation costs should reflect actual contracted routes or per-trip costs, not just vehicle ownership.

Key Metrics Explained

Cost Per Enrolled Student (CPE)

CPE = Total Annual Cost ÷ Students Enrolled

Used for budgeting and fee-setting. Reflects the average cost to serve one slot in the program regardless of attendance.

Cost Per ADA Student

Cost/ADA = Total Annual Cost ÷ Average Daily Attendance

A higher-fidelity measure of per-student cost that accounts for actual attendance patterns. Used in state and federal reporting.

Cost Per Participant Hour (CPH)

CPH = Total Annual Cost ÷ (ADA × Hours/Day × Operating Days)

The most granular cost metric. Allows comparison across programs with different schedules. Often used by funders to assess efficiency.

Staff-to-Student Ratio

Ratio = ADA ÷ (# Directors + # Staff)

Regulatory minimum ratios vary by state and age group. Best practice is 1:10–15 for elementary and 1:12–18 for middle/high school.
